COOKIE POLICY

At Casha (https://cashamx.netlify.app/) we use cookies and similar technologies to ensure the proper functioning of our website, analyze traffic statistically, and enhance your browsing experience. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, the types we may use, and how you can manage or disable them.

  1. What are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that websites store on your browser or device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit them. They are used to remember technical preferences, measure site performance, and understand how users interact with our pages.

  2. Types of Cookies We Use

    Depending on the technical setup and ongoing updates to our website, we may use the following categories of cookies:

    • Strictly Necessary or Technical Cookies

      Essential for the website to function properly, load the interface, remember your language preference (English/Spanish), and maintain browsing security. These cookies do not collect personal data for marketing purposes.

    • Analytics and Performance Cookies (e.g., Google Analytics / server metrics)

      Allow us to count visits, identify the most viewed sections, and measure technical performance in an aggregated, anonymized manner to improve our content.

    • Marketing and Advertising Cookies (e.g., Meta Pixel)

      When active, these help measure the effectiveness of our informational or advertising campaigns and understand interactions stemming from links to external platforms such as WhatsApp.

  3. Third-Party Cookies

    When interacting with third-party services or clicking direct contact links to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.), those external platforms may set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies, over which Casha has no direct control.

  4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

    You retain full control over the use of cookies on your device and can manage them in two ways:

    • Through our Cookie Settings banner

      • Whenever our cookie consent banner is active on the website, you may accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookie categories at any time.

    • Through your web browser

      You can block, restrict, or delete existing cookies by adjusting the privacy settings in your browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, etc.). Please note that disabling technical cookies may affect how the website displays or functions.

  5. Contact and Updates

    Casha may update this Cookie Policy as new technological tools are implemented or due to legal requirements. If you have any questions regarding our use of these technologies, please contact us at contacto.casha@gmail.com or review our Privacy Notice.
